Volunteer Pathways

We offer flexible volunteer opportunities designed to fit your schedule and interests. Whether you have a few hours a week or want to take on a leadership role, there's a place for you.

  • Food Recovery & Harvesting Shifts — Hands-on work at farms and partner locations collecting and harvesting surplus food
  • Logistics & Coordination — Plan routes, manage schedules and conduct deliveries
  • Outreach & Education — Lead workshops, speak at schools, fundraise and advocate for food justice
